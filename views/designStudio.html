Privacy-first creative workspace

Your prototypes and sketches are private drafts. Nothing in this studio is published automatically.

01

Local creative media

Photos and video are previewed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to Fieldnote. Removing a media file clears it from the studio preview.

02

Local draft storage

Code, brand, and drawing drafts are saved only when you choose “Save on this device.” They stay in this browser's local storage and are not uploaded to Fieldnote. Clear saved drafts on shared devices.

03

Secure coding habits

  • Never paste passwords, API keys, or private learner data into a prototype.
  • Validate data on the server and enforce access rules there, too.
  • Render untrusted text as text; avoid eval and unsafe HTML injection.
  • Use HTTPS and collect only the personal data a feature truly needs.
04

Respect creative rights

Use original designs or assets you have permission to use. Keep attribution and license details with third-party fonts, images, icons, and code. Fieldnote Learning content and original brand assets are protected by copyright.
